# EP002 — "The Wave"

**Logline.** Sam tracks down a driver who didn't wave after being let into a lane, and
discovers three hours later that the man is the stonemason coming to template his
benchtops.

## Shooting script

**0:00** — Black. Title card. Sting. **CUT.**

---

**0:02 — SCENE A. INT. HIACE, METROPOLITAN RING ROAD — MORNING**

*The theory scene. Two-shot from the back seat, windscreen blown out. SAM driving, KOSTA in the passenger seat with a coffee.*

> **SAM:** I let him in. Full stop, let him in, gave him the whole gap. Nothing.
> **KOSTA:** Maybe he didn't see.
> **SAM:** He saw. Two fingers off the wheel, that's all it is. That's the whole thing. That's society.
> **KOSTA:** Mm.
> **SAM:** If we're not waving, what are we doing?
> **KOSTA:** *(checking his phone)* Trev's sending the new bloke to template at two.

**CUT.** *(1s sting)*

---

**0:18 — SCENE B. EXT. SERVO, CAMPBELLFIELD — MORNING**

*Wide, 1s: bowsers, a ute, the HiAce parked at an angle. Then geometry.*

*A MAN in his 30s is filling a silver Hilux. SAM approaches, holding his folder.*

> **SAM:** Grey Hilux. Ring Road, about twenty past.
> **MAN:** Yeah?
> **SAM:** I let you in.
> **MAN:** *(beat)* Okay.
> **SAM:** You didn't wave.
> **MAN:** ...Sorry?
> **SAM:** I'm not having a go. I'm just letting you know. Two fingers. Off the wheel. It takes nothing.

*The MAN looks at the bowser. Looks at Sam. Puts the nozzle back.*

> **MAN:** No worries.
> **SAM:** *(satisfied)* Good on you.

**CUT.** *(1s sting)*

---

**0:34 — SCENE C. INT. WORKSHOP — AFTERNOON**

*Wide, 1.5s: roller door up, the assembled carcasses lined along the wall, dust extractor running. Then geometry.*

*JARRAH is holding a clipboard for someone. SAM comes out of the office. The someone turns around. It's the MAN.*

*Hold three seconds. The dust extractor is the only sound.*

> **SAM:** ...You're the new bloke.
> **MAN:** Yeah.

*The MAN goes back to his laser. Measures. Writes something. Doesn't hurry.*

> **MAN:** Can't template today.
> **SAM:** You're standing in front of it.
> **MAN:** Walls are out. I'll need to come back.
> **SAM:** When?
> **MAN:** Three weeks.

---

**0:50 — THE SNAP**

*SAM sets the folder down on the bench. Very calm.*

> **SAM:** This is about the merge.

*Beat. **HOLD — three full seconds.** The MAN packs his laser without any particular attitude.*

> **MAN:** I don't know what that is.
> **SAM:** Two millimetres out is within tolerance. Yours are three at the worst point. I measured them Tuesday.
> **MAN:** Yeah, I'm not arguing.

---

**0:57 — THE BUTTON**

*The MAN walks out under the roller door. Without turning around, he lifts **two fingers off the clipboard.***

*SAM watches him go.*

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.** Sting.

**1:00 — END**
