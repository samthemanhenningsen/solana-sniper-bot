# EP003 — "The Ensuite"

**Logline.** Sam establishes that a tradie never uses the client's ensuite, wins the
argument on a technicality, and is then obliged by his own logic to use it.

## Thread map

| Thread | Planted | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — The toilet rule.** A tradie uses the downstairs toilet. Never the ensuite. | Scene A | Scene C |
| **2 — The seed.** Fiona has given Sam's number to her book club. | Scene A, thrown away over the dishes | Button |

The trap here is different from EP001 and EP002: **Sam builds it himself.** He wins an
argument in Scene B and the victory is the mechanism that destroys him in Scene C. This
is the "farce staircase" from "The Doll" compressed into forty seconds — every step is
individually the most reasonable available move.

---

## Shooting script

**0:00** — Black. Title card. Sting. **CUT.**

---

**0:02 — SCENE A. INT. SAM'S KITCHEN, COBURG — EARLY MORNING**

*Wide, 1.5s: bench, two mugs, BAZZA asleep on the couch in the background in a fluoro vest. Then geometry.*

*NIC is making coffee. SAM is putting his boots on.*

> **SAM:** You use the downstairs one. That's it. That's the rule.
> **NIC:** Is it written down anywhere?
> **SAM:** It doesn't need to be. Nobody wants a bloke in their ensuite.
> **NIC:** *(handing him the coffee, turning to the sink)* Fiona's given your number to her whole book club, by the way.

**CUT.** *(1s sting)*

---

**0:16 — SCENE B. INT. HALLWAY, BRIGHTON — MID-MORNING**

*Wide, 1s: plastic runner down the hall, a door ajar. Then geometry.*

*CRAIG the builder comes out of the main bedroom, doing up his belt. SAM is waiting.*

> **SAM:** That's the ensuite.
> **CRAIG:** Yeah.
> **SAM:** You use the downstairs one, mate.
> **CRAIG:** *(genuinely pleasant)* It's a single-storey house.

*Beat.*

> **SAM:** ...Say that again?
> **CRAIG:** There's no downstairs. It's all downstairs.
> **SAM:** Then there's no ensuite either. It's a toilet. In a bedroom.
> **CRAIG:** *(thinks about it, shrugs, walking off)* She'll be right.

**CUT.** *(1s sting)*

---

**0:34 — SCENE C. INT. HALLWAY / BEDROOM, BRIGHTON — AFTERNOON**

*Same hallway. SAM alone. He looks down the hall. He looks at the bedroom door. We can see the calculation happening and he does not enjoy it.*

*He goes in. Closes the door.*

*Hold on the closed door. Two seconds.*

*The door opens. SAM comes out. FIONA is standing in the hall with a fabric sample.*

> **FIONA:** That's our bedroom.

---

**0:50 — THE SNAP**

*SAM does not flinch. He is not embarrassed. He is explaining.*

> **SAM:** It's a single-storey house. By definition there's no ensuite. There's a toilet, and there's a bedroom that happens to be near it.

*Beat. **HOLD — three full seconds.** FIONA looks at the fabric sample.*

> **FIONA:** *(pleasantly)* I've got book club on Thursday.

---

**0:57 — THE BUTTON**

*SAM opens his mouth. CRAIG passes behind him in the hall, cheerful, not stopping.*

> **CRAIG:** All good?
> **SAM:** *(after a beat)* Yeah, good.

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.** Sting.

**1:00 — END**
