# EP001 — "The Snag"

**Logline.** Sam corrects a volunteer's onion placement at a Bunnings sausage sizzle and
meets him again four hours later, holding the final payment on a Brighton kitchen.

## Thread map

| Thread | Planted | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — The onion directive.** Onion goes under the sausage; it's written down; it's a slip hazard. | Scene A | Button |
| **2 — The seed.** Fiona's dad is doing the handover and signing off. | Scene A, thrown away on an exit | Scene C reveal |
| **Runner — the tape measure.** Sam's tape is missing off his belt. | Scene A, non-verbal | Scene C (he can't verify) + Button |

The tape measure is a series runner, not a third thread — it carries no plot, it only
calibrates Sam and then executes him. (See `01-teardowns.md`: the half-bottle-of-water
thread in "The Doll" does the same job.)

---

## Shooting script

> **CAMERA GEOMETRY** — every two-hander: **A** = over Sam's shoulder onto the other person, **B** = over their shoulder onto Sam. Both roll simultaneously, always. Handheld.

**0:00** — Black. Title card: *MELBOURNE CABINET MAKERS with Enthusiasm*. Music sting. **CUT.**

---

**0:02 — SCENE A. INT. WORKSHOP, THOMASTOWN — MORNING**

*Wide, 1.5s: roller door half up, dust in the light, panel saw running. Then into the geometry.*

*SAM is at the bench. KOSTA is signing something. JARRAH is on his phone.*

> **SAM:** Onion goes under. It's a directive. It's written down.
> **KOSTA:** Nobody's read it.
> **SAM:** It's a slip hazard.
> **JARRAH:** Is it?
> **SAM:** A bloke went down in the timber aisle.

*SAM pats his belt for his tape measure. It isn't there. He looks at JARRAH. JARRAH doesn't look up.*

> **KOSTA:** *(going, over his shoulder, half out the roller door)* Brighton at four. Fiona's old man's signing off.

**CUT.** *(1s sting)*

---

**0:16 — SCENE B. EXT. BUNNINGS CARPARK — MIDDAY**

*Wide, 1s: the trestle, the urn, a queue of six. Then geometry.*

*The VOLUNTEER (70s, mild, apron, tongs) has a sausage on bread. Onion on top.*

> **VOLUNTEER:** Onion?
> **SAM:** Under.
> **VOLUNTEER:** ...Sorry?
> **SAM:** Under the sausage. It's the directive.
> **VOLUNTEER:** I've always done it on top.
> **SAM:** I know.

*Beat. SAM doesn't take it.*

> **SAM:** Can you do it again?
> **VOLUNTEER:** Do it—
> **SAM:** I'll wait. I'm doing you a favour, mate. Someone goes down, that's on you.

*The VOLUNTEER remakes it. The queue watches. Nobody says anything.*

> **VOLUNTEER:** Righto.

**CUT.** *(1s sting)*

---

**0:32 — SCENE C. INT. CLIENT'S KITCHEN, BRIGHTON — AFTERNOON**

*Wide, 1.5s: plastic sheeting, new joinery, a sink still on the floor. Then geometry.*

*SAM enters with the folder. FIONA is there. A man in a polo has his back to us, opening and closing a drawer.*

> **FIONA:** Sam — this is my dad. He did kitchens his whole life.

*The man turns. It's the VOLUNTEER. Hold two seconds. Neither says the thing.*

> **SAM:** ...The onion man.
> **RON:** Ron.

*RON goes back to the drawer. Runs a finger down the gap at the edge. Doesn't look up.*

> **RON:** That's three.
> **SAM:** That's two. Two mil's the tolerance.
> **RON:** I'll get a tape.
> **SAM:** I've got a—

*SAM reaches for his belt. Nothing there. He keeps his hand there a moment too long.*

---

**0:50 — THE SNAP**

*RON waits. Pleasant. Doesn't help. **HOLD THE SILENCE — three full seconds.** SAM does not move.*

> **SAM:** ...It might be three.

*RON nods slowly and closes the drawer.*

> **RON:** Onion on top from now on, is it?

---

**0:57 — THE BUTTON**

*SAM opens his mouth. Nothing comes out. Behind him, through the doorway, JARRAH walks past carrying a panel — **Sam's tape measure on his belt.***

**HARD CUT TO BLACK.** Sting.

**1:00 — END**
